How the Platform Works

Learn about our secure, decentralized funding process.

1. Connect Your Wallet: Start by connecting your crypto wallet. This becomes your secure identity on the platform.

2. Browse Campaigns: Explore live campaigns, their goals, and real-time progress.

3. Complete KYC Verification: Before donating or creating campaigns, verify your identity for safety and compliance.

4. Donate or Create: Verified users can donate securely or launch their own campaigns using smart contracts

5. Track Everything: All transactions appear instantly on the blockchain and a transaction view is provided in your ptofile for web access.
